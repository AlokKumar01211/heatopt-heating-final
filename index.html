Model assumptions and data sources

Home heat loss comes from calibrated Boston, Atlanta and Montreal building archetypes and is adjusted for floor area, construction year, occupants and thermostat setting. The model uses 8,760 hours of city-specific typical-year weather and temperature-dependent equipment curves. This browser calculator approximates the project's full ISO 13790 5R1C model; it does not re-run that dynamic model for each entered home.

Annual cost includes purchased energy. The optimization compares annualized installed heat pump cost over 15 years at a 5% discount rate. The balanced selection adds $190 per tonne CO₂e to annualized cost as an explicit scenario assumption. Existing backup equipment is treated as already installed; new backup installation costs and rebates are not included. Equipment performance curves include interpolated or extrapolated temperatures, and sizing scales reference performance curves rather than guaranteeing that exact equipment size is sold.

Wood prices may rely on historical/proxy data. Fuel and grid factors follow the uploaded EIA / EPA / eGRID / Hydro-Québec data and the project files. No hourly marginal grid emissions or full lifecycle refrigerant impacts are included.

Screening estimate, not installation advice. Actual heat pump sizing needs an on-site heating-load assessment.
